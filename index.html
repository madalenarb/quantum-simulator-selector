This page is the prototype mentioned in Section 6.3 of the MSc dissertation Accelerating Quantum Circuit Simulation with NVIDIA GPUs and tools editing, Madalena Barros, Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade de Lisboa, 2026. It encodes both levels of the selection framework and the results of Chapter 5 as rules. Sections, figures and tables cited here are those of the dissertation. Times are the values plotted in Chapter 5, measured on Deucalion and on the i9-13900KS platform in double precision; estimates and interpolations are marked wherever they appear.
