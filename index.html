Quantum Simulator Selector

Answer a few questions about your circuits and your hardware, or read your circuit from an OpenQASM file. The selector walks the two-level framework of Section 5.8, first the simulation method, then the backend, and returns the fastest configuration measured in this dissertation, with every backend measured at your size and a run configuration to start from.
